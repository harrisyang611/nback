**Subject: n-back data columns — what each one means**

Hi [name],

Here's a quick guide to the n-back columns in the Qualtrics export. Each row is one participant.

- **nBackParticipantId** — the ID they typed on the start screen.
- **nBackLevel** — the difficulty level they played (1, 2, or 3).
- **nBackHits** — number of times a letter *should* have been tapped and they tapped it (correct).
- **nBackMiss** — number of times a letter should have been tapped but they didn't (missed it).
- **nBackFA** — number of times they tapped when they shouldn't have (tapped by mistake).
- **nBackAccuracy** — overall percentage they got right.
- **nBackAvgRT** — their average tap speed, in milliseconds (lower = faster).

In short: high **Hits** and **Accuracy** with low **Miss** and **FA** means they did well.

One important thing: the scores are only saved if the participant **finishes the whole survey to the very last page.** The game running isn't enough on its own — if they close the browser after the game but before the end of the survey, their scores won't come through in the download. So please make sure everyone completes the full survey.

On the task itself: it can run with **letters** or with **pictures**. If you'd like the training to use pictures, just send me the list of images you want to use and I can add them easily. If letters are fine for your purposes, no action needed.

If any of these columns come out empty for everyone, let me know — there's a Qualtrics layout setting that can cause it, and it's a quick fix.
